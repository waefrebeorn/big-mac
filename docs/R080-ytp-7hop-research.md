# R080 — YTP Source Material: 7-Hop Kevin Bacon Research

## The Method
Each "lily pad" is a research hop. Jump from source to source, building connections.
7 hops → convergent truth about what makes iconic YTP material.

## Hop 1: Wikipedia → Canonical YTP Sources
**Source**: Wikipedia "YouTube Poop" article
**Finding**: The most iconic YTP sources are:
- Hotel Mario / Zelda CD-i (Faces of Evil, Wand of Gamelon)
- Michael Rosen (children's poet)
- SpongeBob SquarePants ("Shanghaied" → "The Sky Had a Weegee")
- Super Mario World cartoon
- Adventures of Sonic the Hedgehog (→ "Pingas" meme)
- Mario Bros 3 cartoon (first YTP ever: "I'd Say He's Hot On Our Tail")

## Hop 2: Project Crusade Wiki → YTP Universe
**Source**: projectcrusade.fandom.com
**Finding**: YTP trends by era: Zelda CD-i → Mario cartoons → Michael Rosen → SpongeBob. Tennis format creates chains of remixes.

## Hop 3: YouChew Forums → Tennis Culture
**Source**: video-tennis.com / yellowtealpurple.net
**Finding**: YTP Tennis = back-and-forth editing. 6-round standard. Classic vs Technical schism. Key figures: conradslater, RabbitSnore, RAKninja, MycroProcessor.

## Hop 4: Reddit → Community Knowledge
**Source**: r/YouTubePoop, r/starterpacks
**Finding**: Classic YTP starter pack includes: Michael Rosen, Paula Deen, Hotel Mario, Sonic, SpongeBob. "Pingas" is the most recognized YTP word.

## Hop 5: Archive.org → Acquired Sources
**Source**: Internet Archive collections
**Finding**: Massive collections available: Caillou, Barney, Thomas, Sesame Street, Arthur (20 seasons), Bob Ross, Billy Mays infomercials, 90s commercials.

## Hop 6: YouTube → Modern YTP
**Source**: YouTube searches
**Finding**: Shrek, SpongeBob "Band Geeks", Arthur readings, Donkey's Song. Compilation videos are goldmines.

## Hop 7: Big Mac Engine → Transcription
**Source**: whisper.cpp integration
**Finding**: 31s for 5min video. Transcript-first editing = text is cheaper than pixels. Audio analysis finds best moments for effects.

## Convergent Truth
**Iconic YTP sources share 3 properties:**
1. **Recognizable** — viewers instantly know the source
2. **Repetitive** — catchphrases, musical loops, visual gags
3. **Absurd potential** — serious content made silly, or silly content made serious

**The best YTP sources are:**
- Children's media (innocent → absurd contrast)
- Bad voice acting (CD-i games, old cartoons)
- Repetitive dialogue (commercials, educational videos)
- Emotional peaks (anger, excitement, sadness → earrape/stutter)
