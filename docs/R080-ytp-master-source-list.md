## Key YTP Techniques from Research

### Sentence Mixing
- "Pingas" from Sonic "Boogey-Mania" (snooping as usual → pi-ngas)
- "The Sky Had a Weegee" from SpongeBob "Shanghaied"
- "I'd say he's hot on our tail" from Mario Bros 3

### Stutter/Repeat
- Short phrases repeated 5-10x
- Musical beats synced to visual stutters

### Pitch Shift
- Chipmunk (2.0-2.5x) for absurdity
- Demon (0.3-0.4x) for menace

### Earrape
- Volume 10-20x on dramatic moments
- Meme SFX overlay (vine boom, etc.)
