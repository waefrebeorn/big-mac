# R082 — YTP/3D Poop Character Asset Master List

## Strategy
Focus on characters from:
1. **Older consoles** (NES, SNES, Genesis, GBA) — 2D sprites, tiny file sizes, instantly loadable
3. **N64/PS1 era** — low-poly 3D models, perfect for 3D poop
4. **Retro indie** — Undertale, Deltarune, Friday Night Funkin', etc.
5. **Most popular = most recognizable = best YTP material**

All assets from spriters-resource.com and models-resource.com (free, personal use).

## NOTES

- **2D sprites** are PNG files, typically 16x16 to 256x256 pixels. A full character set is ~500KB-2MB.
- **3D models** from N64/PS1 era are typically 500-5000 triangles, ~50KB-500KB each.
- **Modern 3D models** (GameCube+) are 5000-50000 triangles, ~500KB-5MB each.
- **Total storage for full library**: ~500MB-2GB (fits easily on any drive).
- **Loading time**: 2D sprites load instantly. N64 models load in <10ms. Modern models load in <100ms.
- **All assets are free for personal/non-commercial use** from spriters-resource.com and models-resource.com.
- **Perfect for YTP**: These are the most recognizable characters from gaming history.
- **Perfect for 3D poop**: Low-poly models are easy to pose, animate, and render.
